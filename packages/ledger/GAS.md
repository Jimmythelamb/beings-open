# Gas report (local Hardhat network)

Measured by `npm run gas -w @beings/ledger` on the in-process Hardhat network (chain ID 114). Solc 0.8.24, optimizer 200 runs, EVM london. Numbers are gas used per transaction (EVM gas including the 21,000 base and calldata); multiply by the gas price for cost. The scenario is the core "tally" vector: 8 printed ballots, 6 registered, quorum 1, 2 questions, 5 recorded ballots, 2 corrections, 1 spoiled ballot. Values on Coston2 can differ slightly (storage-touch rules are the same, but the calldata and chain parameters are not identical). `Generated by the script; do not edit by hand.`
